HOW TO PLAY

THRUSTW / ↑ — accelerate forward. S / ↓ — reverse thrust.
ROTATEA / ← and D / → — turn your ship.
MOMENTUMYour ship drifts — it keeps moving when you stop thrusting. Fly with your velocity, not against it.

FUELThrusting burns fuel. Run dry and you'll coast on momentum alone — plan ahead.
COLLECTFly near fuel cells, energy cells, salvage, repair kits & rare cores to pull them in.
COLLISIONSAsteroids hurt more at high speed. Dodge, don't ram.
