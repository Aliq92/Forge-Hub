How It Works

Attractors pull particles. Stars, planets, heavy cores and anchors bend the paths of nearby particles with gravity.

Particles keep their momentum. Once moving, they continue in a straight line until gravity curves them.

Spin creates orbital motion. Give a spawned cloud spin and it will settle into rings, spirals, and discs instead of collapsing to a point.

Stronger gravity bends trajectories more. Raise Gravity Strength to see sharper slingshots and tighter orbits.

Multiple attractors create chaos. Two or three moving bodies interacting produce complex, unpredictable structures.

Pause anytime, drag attractors with Alt or the Move tool, then resume to see the whole structure reorganize.
