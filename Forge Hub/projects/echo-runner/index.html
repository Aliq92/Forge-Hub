HOW TO PLAY

  • A / D or ←/→ — move
  • SPACE / W / ↑ — jump
  • E — interact with switches
  • R — start / stop recording an echo
  • Q — reset the current attempt
  • ESC — pause
  • Touch devices — on-screen buttons appear automatically

Record your own movement, then let it play back as a translucent echo. Reset yourself to the start and your echo will repeat everything you did — freeing you to be somewhere else. Hold plates, flip switches, and cross doors together with your past selves. Every room has a clear solution: the challenge is sequencing, not memorizing hidden rules.
