How Beat Foundry works

Step Sequencer
Each row is an instrument. Click a square to make it play on that beat, 1 through 16.
BPM
Beats per minute — how fast the loop plays. Drag the slider while playing to hear it change instantly.
Swing
Delays every other step slightly for a laid-back, human feel. Doesn't change the overall speed.
Mute / Solo
Mute silences a track. Solo isolates it — while any track is soloed, only soloed tracks play.
Patterns
Four independent loops (A–D). Switching while playing waits for the bar to end, so it stays in time.
Bass Notes
Click a bass step to select it, then pick its note from the strip below the grid.
Effects
Delay repeats echoes in time with the beat. Space adds ambience. The Filter can sweep the whole mix darker or brighter.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • H Percussion

Welcome to Beat Foundry

  1. Press PLAY. A demo groove is already loaded.
  2. Tap glowing squares on/off in the sequencer to change the beat.
  3. Tap the drum pads below to play live over the loop.
  4. Drag BPM to speed up or slow down.
  5. Try a preset for an instant new vibe.
