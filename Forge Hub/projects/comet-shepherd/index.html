COMET SHEPHERD

Momentum is destiny. Gravity is your only ally.

Mouse/Touch to aim & correct · Arrow/AD nudge · Space impulse · E preview · Esc pause

HOW TO PLAY

Momentum

Your comet drifts forever on its current heading unless gravity or a correction changes it. You never steer directly — you nudge.

Course Correction

Click / touch and drag away from the comet, then release to fire a small thrust in that direction. Arrow keys / A & D give cheap, precise lateral nudges. Every correction spends Correction Energy.

Trajectory Preview

Hold E or drag to reveal a projected path bent by nearby gravity. Use it to judge whether you'll clear a planet — or slingshot around it.

Gravity Assists

Skim close to a planet without colliding and its gravity will whip you onto a faster new heading. Watch for the SLINGSHOT feedback.

Ice & Heat

Sunlight melts you. Ice Integrity is your health — it drops from heat, collisions and hazards. Stay cold, collect ice fragments, and mind the CRITICAL warning.

Reaching the Gate

Every system has a deep-space gate near its outer edge. Reach it to bank an upgrade and warp to a harder, stranger system.
